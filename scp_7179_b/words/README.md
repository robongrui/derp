# The episode's word list

`2of12.txt` is a word list that episode 0.5, SCP_7179_B, uses.

It is **2of12** from Alan Beale's **12dicts** word lists, version 6.0.2
(`American/2of12.txt` in `12dicts-6.0.2.zip` from
<http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/>), cut down to the entries of plain
lowercase letters a to z (no hyphens, apostrophes or capitals). SHA-256 of
the archive `64ac1d35acb66b550c7ebc56e080b62e0bad8f5984d72059dc2e05ac48780e52`, of its
`American/2of12.txt` `235f1d8bb6155ffae3797b514e55916ae8905acb77ec08bb643cf0440e84ff1a`;
39,581 of its 41,242 entries kept.

> The 12dicts lists were compiled by Alan Beale. I explicitly release them
> to the public domain, but request acknowledgment of their use.

Thank you, Alan Beale. The list is public domain; see
[THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).

It also goes into the episode's WAD (`library.wad`) as the lump
`LIBWORDS`, one word to a line, leaving out the single letters other than
"a" and "i" (the list has each letter as a word, its name).
